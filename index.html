About

Design that starts from real people.

I’m Armn Rezei, a product designer and computer engineer. Every day I see people who struggle with technology and get frustrated by digital products that confuse them. That taught me most problems come from bad design, not technology itself.

I work with businesses that either don’t have a proper digital presence yet, or were previously let down by unprofessional work, building websites and products that are both technically solid and genuinely usable for their customers.
